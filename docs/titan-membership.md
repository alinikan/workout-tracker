# Titan Membership Decision

**Research checked October 25, 2026. All amounts CAD; no purchase made.**

## Recommendation

For one Wednesday class each week plus home strength, try the eligible free introduction first.
If you like it, the **$300 adult ten-class pass** is the best initial fit **provided** Titan confirms
that the introduction/next class are eligible and the pass remains valid over at least ten weeks.
If expiry is shorter, this recommendation must be recalculated. Avoid a long contract while testing
whether this routine fits work, recovery and actual attendance.

If the introduction is encouraging but ten visits still feel uncertain, one or two $50 drop-ins
limit the initial commitment. Using those two drop-ins before the ten-pack instead of after it
still totals $400 base in the twelve-paid-visit illustration below.

## Credit Value Is Not Cash Paid

One fully used pass works out to $30/class. Four or five monthly visits consume $120 or $150 of credits,
compared with $210 for month-to-month unlimited. You still pay **$300 upfront**, not $120.

Seven visits consume $210 of pass credits: equal to the monthly rate. At **eight or more actual visits
per billing month**, $210 unlimited is cheaper than $30/class credit use. That is approximately twice
weekly, but months and billing cycles vary. Establish attendance before changing membership.

A Monday-start 90-day block includes thirteen Wednesdays. If classes all run and the first visit is free,
twelve visits are paid. One ten-pack plus two $50 drop-ins is **$400 cash**. Buying two ten-packs is
**$600 cash with eight credits left**, not $360 cash merely because twelve credits were used.
Three $210 billing cycles total **$630 base**. This is an illustrative comparison, not a quote for
specific billing dates, holidays, fees or class eligibility. Buying a second pass may make sense if
continuing beyond the block, but unused credits and expiry matter.

The six-month rate saves $30 per month versus month-to-month but creates a commitment beyond a 90-day
trial of the routine. The annual rate has still more exposure. Neither is the starting recommendation.

## Confirm Before Paying

- Does the adult ten-class pass cover both Introduction and Beginners/Intermediate Muay Thai?
- When does it expire, when does that clock start, and can unused credits carry forward?
- Is the planned first introduction eligible for the advertised free trial?
- Are tax, registration, equipment or administration charges additional?
- What are the renewal, cancellation, notice and freeze terms?
- Will planned holiday-week classes run, and is advance booking required?

These details were not established by the public price PDF/catalogue. No expiry, no-fee promise,
automatic free booking or cancel-anytime claim should be inferred.

## Class Track

The [official Muay Thai schedule](https://titantrainingcentre.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Titan-Training-Centre-Schedule-2026-2.pdf)
lists Wednesday Introduction at **6:15 pm, Room #2 Front Entrance**, and Beginners/Intermediate at
**6:15 pm, Room #1 Main Room**. The [program FAQ](https://titantrainingcentre.com/program/muay-thai-classes/)
describes normal classes as one hour and first-session glove provision. Confirm current arrangements.

Stay in Introduction while it is useful. Choose the switch with the instructor; do not advance by
calendar alone. Saturday's published class requires four months' experience, not merely finishing
the app's 90-day block. Advanced sparring is a different class and is never automatically enabled.
